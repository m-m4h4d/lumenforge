What's inside

A real CAD workflow, scoped to one beautiful object.

Every parameter updates the mesh live, so you can see the shape, the texture and the light together while you design.

Silhouette profiles

Cone, bell, bulge, hourglass, ovoid and tiered curves, with precise height, top and base radius in millimetres.

Surface topology

Pleated, sinusoidal, diamond, bamboo, origami and cellular textures, with twist and depth you can dial in.

Socket fitters

Spoke rings sized for E27, E26, E14 and UNO, or skip the fitter for vase-mode prints and pendant builds.

Light simulation

Switch the internal bulb on, change colour temperature, and see the shade's glow and the light it casts on the floor.

Print diagnostics

Overhang analysis, a support-free printability check, build volume, and estimates for PLA weight and print time. Use the section cut to inspect wall thickness.

Watertight STL export

Download binary or ASCII STL, generated in the browser and ready for Cura, PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio or Orca.

How it works

From idea to print bed in four steps.

Shape

Start from a preset or a blank profile and set the dimensions.

Texture

Choose a surface pattern and tune its frequency, depth and twist.

Light

Fit the socket, turn on the bulb and check the glow from any angle.

Print

Export the STL, slice it, and print in your favourite translucent filament.

Presets

Start from a design that already works.

One click loads a complete set of parameters. Change anything from there.
